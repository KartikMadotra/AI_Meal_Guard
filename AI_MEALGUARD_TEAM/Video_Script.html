How the AI Works

⏱ 1:10 – 1:35 (25s)
🎥
VISUAL: Rishit faces camera from home (medium close-up). While speaking, Shaurya overlays the AI pipeline flowchart diagram, YOLO detection screenshot, and model metrics on screen.
🎤 RISHIT — Documentation & Research
"The AI behind MealGuard is YOLOv8 Instance Segmentation — trained on 50 Indian food classes using the Indian Thali Dataset. It doesn't just draw boxes — it creates pixel-perfect masks that tell us the exact area each food covers."
Image → YOLOv8 → Masks → Weight → Nutrition → Score
"We convert pixel area into estimated grams using density factors, look up nutrition from the IFCT 2017 database, and compare against ICMR-NIN benchmarks for the student's age group. The scoring formula is: Protein 40% + Energy 40% + Variety 20%."
📊
SHOW ON SCREEN: Flash the metrics — Precision: 91.2% · Recall: 89.5% · mAP@50: 92.4%. Use backend/results/MaskF1_curve.png and confusion_matrix.png.
"Our model achieves 91.2% precision and 92.4% mAP on the test set."

Production Guide — For Everyone

📷 How Each Person Should Record

📱 Camera Setup

Use your phone's front camera in landscape mode (horizontal). Prop it at eye level using books or a box. Resolution: 1080p minimum.

💡 Lighting

Sit facing a window for natural light. No lights behind you (avoid silhouette). Record during daytime for best quality.

🎙️ Audio

Record in a quiet room. No fan noise. Use earphone mic if available — holds closer to your mouth. Speak clearly and at a natural pace.

🖼️ Background

Clean wall or bookshelf behind you. No messy backgrounds. If unavailable, use slight blur/bokeh from phone portrait mode.

👕 Framing

Head + shoulders visible. Center yourself. Leave some space above your head. Look at the camera lens, not the screen.

🔄 Multiple Takes

Record each section 3 times. Pick the best one. It's okay to make mistakes — Shaurya will edit. Send all takes.
