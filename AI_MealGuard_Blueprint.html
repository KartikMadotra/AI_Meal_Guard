1. Core Objective

Main question: Is the meal nutritionally appropriate for the selected student profile, and what can be improved? MealGuard combines computer vision, approximate portion estimation, nutrition data, age-specific benchmarks, explainable scoring and recommendations.

6. Food & Quantity Detection

Food Detection

Use a computer-vision model such as YOLO for relevant school-meal food classes.

Segmentation

Prefer segmentation where possible because portion estimation needs the food region, not only a bounding box.

Quantity

Use a standardized plate, fixed camera geometry and calibration to estimate approximate mass or volume.

Scientific limitation: A normal RGB camera does not directly weigh food. Report values as estimates such as ~80 g, validate against a scale, and publish your measured error.

8. Nutrition Calculation

For each detected food, quantity is combined with the nutrition database to estimate nutrient contribution. The system then sums contributions across the entire meal.

Energy

Estimated kcal from all detected food portions.

Protein

Primary nutrient metric, especially useful for explaining deficiencies.

Micronutrients

Potentially iron, calcium and other nutrients supported by the chosen reference data.

11. “WHY?” Explanation

Why did the meal receive this score?

  • Show selected age group.
  • Show estimated nutrient amount.
  • Show the selected benchmark.
  • Show percentage coverage.
  • Identify the main contributing food.
  • Explain the failed or weak criterion.
Example: Protein is below the selected benchmark. Dal is the main detected protein source. Consider increasing the pulse/protein component.

18. Privacy & Scientific Guardrails

  • Use fake/demo student records for the competition.
  • Keep face identification disabled by default.
  • Do not claim that visual analysis proves food safety or contamination status.
  • Use “estimated quantity” and “estimated nutrient content”.
  • Do not present a project-defined score as an official government score unless it is actually sourced.
  • Record model uncertainty and measurement error where possible.
